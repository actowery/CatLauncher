# Manual test checklist

1. Craft **Cat Launcher** at a machining table after Machining research, then equip it and verify its mechanical handling sound.
2. Verify a tinted cat is visible and rotates throughout its 24-tile flight.
3. Confirm impact damage/radius match a frag grenade and friendly fire remains possible.
4. Confirm central and radial blood is cleanable, while cat fragments fade and cannot be selected.
5. Set survivor chance to 10% in Mod Settings. Confirm a survivor appears beyond the blast radius, has a minor injury, plays the reward sound, and matches the launched cat's tint.
6. Set bloodiness to 0%, 100%, and 300%; verify the blood and fragment intensity changes accordingly.
7. Save during projectile flight, reload, and confirm it still renders and detonates.
