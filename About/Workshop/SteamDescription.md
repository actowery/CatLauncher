[h1]Cat Launcher[/h1]

Launch cats. Regret nothing.

Cat Launcher is a standalone RimWorld weapon mod for [b]1.6 and 1.7 unstable[/b]. Research Machining, craft the launcher at a machining table, and fire spinning cat shells with frag-grenade-equivalent impact damage.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]24-tile range, 2.5-second warmup, and 3-second cooldown.
[*]Every shot launches a spinning cat in a random orange, dark, cream, or brown coat.
[*]Vanilla-style cleanable blood, plus short-lived non-interactable cat-fragment visuals at impact.
[*]A rare survivor-cat chance: survivors appear safely outside the resolved blast, receive a minor injury, and keep the exact coat of the cat that was launched.
[*]Mod settings for survivor chance (default 1%) and bloodiness (default 100%).
[*]No runtime dependencies.
[/list]

[h2]Balance[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires Machining research.
[*]Costs 80 steel, 4 components, and 50 chemfuel.
[*]Uses a 1.9-tile Bomb explosion, matching vanilla frag-grenade radius and damage behavior.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Supports RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7 unstable.
[*]No ordinary shot consumes, harms, or spawns a real cat.
[*]Removing the mod is safe; a rare survivor reverts to normal vanilla cat appearance.
[/list]
